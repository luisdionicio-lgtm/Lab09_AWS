# Design
Lumbre is an image workspace for a photographer reviewing a session on a laptop. Operate mode, with photography carrying the identity. User delegated theme and design.
Grounded directions considered: contact sheet; archive cabinet; photo festival catalog; museum collection interface; travel journal; film editing desk; studio booking board. Selected museum collection interface (assigned fourth): quiet steel-blue navigation, broad white gallery, one oversized typographic collection title, images with small utilitarian captions. No simulated photography or fake upload results.
Palette: canvas #f3f5f7, paper #ffffff, ink #172c3b, secondary #526574, brand #245c83, border #d7e0e6, danger #a52e37.
Typography: self-hosted Manrope for headings and controls, system sans fallback; body 16px, heading 64px desktop / 40px mobile.
Layout: top navigation; split introduction and upload area; toolbar; responsive image grid; accessible preview dialog. Actual user images are the gallery content; empty state is explicit.
Challenger disciplines retained: annual plate section's image prominence; botanical folio's consistent image scale; calm spacing. Decorative stamp/crosshairs are declined because they obscure operating controls.
Motion: short user-triggered progress and dialog transitions, reduced motion supported. Touch controls at least 44px, hover gated by device capability.
